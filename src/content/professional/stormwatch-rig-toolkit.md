Brought on as the rigging specialist for a mid-size studio team, working alongside animators and the tools department to standardize how every character entered production.

The toolkit auto-generates control rigs from a tagged base skeleton, handles space-switching setups for weapons and props, and exports directly into the studio's in-house engine format — replacing a manual process that previously required a rigger's full attention per character.
