A short collaboration on a single hero shot: a stylized shoreline that needed a shader capable of foam, depth-based color, and a painterly ripple pattern without relying on expensive real-time reflections.

Handled the shader graph and material logic; the environment art, lighting, and final scene composition came from the collaborator credited above.
